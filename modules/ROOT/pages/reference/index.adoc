== Your app

Every app on Thinkube is described by `thinkube.yaml`, one short file at the root of its repository. It says only what you know about your app: its containers, how each is built, the port it listens on, where requests go, and the services it needs, such as a database. At deploy time the platform generates everything Kubernetes needs from it. xref:apps/index.adoc#_thinkube_yaml_your_app_without_the_kubernetes[Thinkube GitOps] explains what it replaces.
